# Sanad Privacy Policy & Sovereign Data Framework

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Baseline  
**Classification:** Privacy Specification & Human Rights Framework  

---

## 1. Foundational Privacy Commitment

Sanad is built for Palestinian blind users who operate under heightened physical and digital surveillance risks. Our privacy framework is based on **Data Sovereignty by Design**:

1. **Zero Continuous Tracking:** We never collect, track, or store continuous user location, browsing habits, or behavioral telemetry.
2. **Zero Audio Retention:** Voice recordings are converted to text in volatile memory and instantly overwritten. No voice audio files are saved to device storage or transmitted for model training.
3. **No Monetization or Data Sharing:** User data is never sold, shared with advertising networks, or transferred to third-party commercial entities.
4. **Local Address Book Sovereignty:** Sanad does not upload the user's phone contacts to the cloud. Contact lookups and alias resolutions occur against local contact IDs or securely hashed internal tokens.

---

## 2. User Rights & Data Controls

- **Right to Audio Transparency:** Users can request an immediate spoken summary of recent actions and audit entries via the voice command *"شو مسجل عندك؟"* (What do you have recorded?).
- **Right to Complete Erasure:** The command *"احذف كل بياناتي"* (Delete all my data) triggers an immediate cryptographic erasure of local keys and purges all pseudonymized database records associated with the user account.
- **Physical Inspection Defense (Checkpoint Mode):** Blind users can quickly trigger a local app reset via a discreet hardware button sequence, ensuring no sensitive contact nicknames or communication history remain visible or extractable during device inspection.

---

## 3. Compliance & Ethics Oversight

The privacy architecture is reviewed in partnership with local Palestinian disability advocacy groups and digital rights organizations to ensure adherence to international human rights standards and ethical AI guidelines.
